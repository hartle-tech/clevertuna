The material

Glass built from three painted layers — body, specular rim, lift — over content that is genuinely there. No backdrop-filter: the content behind the glass is real, so a live blur would cost paint without changing the reading.

Glass only needs something real behind it, so the material is painted rather than filtered — three layers, no live blur, and it reads the same at a fraction of the paint.
